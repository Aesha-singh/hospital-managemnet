Built for the ward floor

Everything a care team needs during a shift, on one screen.

Live vital signs

Heart rate, SpO₂, blood pressure, temperature, respiration and ECG, updated every three seconds with trend lines.

Automatic triage

Patients are graded Stable, Serious or Critical from their combined readings, so the sickest are always at the top.

Sensor reliability

Every device gets a reliability score. Loose leads and sudden glitches are flagged instead of raising false alarms.

Resource management

ICU beds, ventilators, blood units and staff are assigned as patients' needs change, and shortages are shown straight away.

Safety checks

Resources are only committed when every admitted patient can still be treated, checked continuously.

Shift reports

Download a full handover report of patients, sensors, resources and events in one click.

How it works

From admission to handover in three steps.

  1. 1

    Admit and connect

    Register the patient and attach the sensors they need. Monitoring starts immediately.

  2. 2

    Monitor continuously

    Readings stream in, triage updates on its own and alerts reach the team the moment something changes.

  3. 3

    Act on priorities

    Critical patients get beds, equipment and staff first, and the shift report captures everything at handover.
